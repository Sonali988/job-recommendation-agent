# Personas — YuvaMitra

Per the approved plan (Q2 = A), this iteration defines one primary persona: the Youth (career-seeker). The AI Agent is treated as system behavior surfaced to this persona rather than as a separate persona; its autonomous actions are written from the youth's perspective (Q5 = A).

---

## Persona 1: Aarav — The Career-Seeking Youth (Primary)

**Role**: End user of the YuvaMitra platform.

**Snapshot**
- Age range: late teens to mid-twenties.
- Context: a MY Bharat youth trying to move toward a concrete career objective (e.g., "become a full-stack developer", "get into data analytics").
- Tech comfort: comfortable with mobile/web apps; not a domain expert in careers or skills planning.
- Language: uses English in this iteration (interface is i18n-ready for Indian languages later).

**Goals & Motivations**
- Set a clear career goal and understand what it takes to get there.
- Know exactly which skills are missing and what to do next.
- Follow a concrete, ordered plan rather than guessing.
- Discover relevant opportunities (courses, programs, openings) without hunting for them.
- Stay motivated by seeing measurable progress over time.

**Frustrations / Pain Points**
- Career advice is generic and not tailored to the individual profile.
- Hard to know which skill to learn first or what the highest-impact next step is.
- Loses track of progress and deadlines; misses opportunities.
- Doesn't want to re-enter information or dig through menus.

**Needs from YuvaMitra**
- A dashboard showing the goal and overall progress at a glance.
- A profile/case that remembers education, skills, interests, goals, activities, and history.
- AI-driven assessment, skill-gap analysis, a prioritised learning roadmap, and clear next-best actions.
- A proactive agent that watches progress, deadlines, opportunities, and inactivity, and nudges with personalised alerts.
- A conversational agent for questions in natural language.

**Success Criteria (from the youth's view)**
- "I understand my gaps and my plan."
- "I always know my single best next action."
- "The app tells me about relevant opportunities and deadlines before I miss them."
- "My progress and history are remembered when I come back."
